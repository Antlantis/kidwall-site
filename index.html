Parental controls you run from your phone.

KidWall puts you in charge of your kids' iPhones and iPads. Pause apps, set daily limits and bedtimes, block social media and websites that aren't safe for kids, and get an alert when they arrive at places like school. It's built on Apple's own Screen Time technology.

Free download · 7-day free trial of Premium · Requires iOS 18 or later

Run it from your own phone

KidWall runs on your child's device and is approved with your Apple Account, so it can't simply be deleted. You pick the apps to block, limit or allow once on your kid's device, then pause, give extra time and change limits, bedtime and websites from your phone.

Pause and extra time

  • A card for each kid's device shows whether apps are open, paused, at bedtime or out of time for today
  • Pause all apps instantly, for dinner, homework or bedtime
  • Your kid taps "Ask for more time". You answer +15, +30, +60 min or No

Bedtime and limits

  • A daily limit on the apps you choose, with a warning before time runs out
  • Bedtime with everything off except the apps you allow, and a different time each night if you like
  • Block apps and whole categories 24/7, like social media or games
  • A web filter that uses Apple's filter plus KidWall's lists of social media and other websites that aren't safe for kids. Other browsers are hidden so kids can't get around it

Places and alerts

  • An alert when your kid arrives at or leaves places you set, like home or school
  • See the device on a map when you turn location on
  • Alerts when a limit is reached, bedtime starts or your kid tries to open something blocked, plus an Activity feed
  • Add a second parent phone with the same family code

Private by design

You're trusting an app with your kids' phones. Here is exactly what KidWall does and doesn't do with your family's data.

Encrypted with your family code

Your kids' names, rules, places, locations and alerts are encrypted with your family code before they leave the device (end-to-end, AES-256-GCM, synced through Apple's iCloud). We can't read them, and neither can Apple or anyone else without your code. A few technical details aren't encrypted so the devices can find their messages, like a random device ID and the type and time of each command or alert; the privacy policy lists them.

No ads, no tracking

  • No ads or ad networks
  • No tracking or analytics SDKs
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • Never sold or shared

No reading messages

KidWall can't read chats in any app. It doesn't collect message contents, browsing history, keystrokes, photos, contacts or per-app usage history.

Apple's App Store privacy label

The App Store lists Data Not Linked to You (Precise Location, Name, Device ID, Other Usage Data), used for App Functionality.

Also off by default: if you choose to send alerts to your own ntfy or webhook address, those alerts go there without KidWall's encryption. Full details are in the privacy policy.

Simple pricing. One plan for the whole family.

Questions parents ask

Can my kid delete KidWall?

Not on their own. KidWall is approved with your Apple Account through Apple's Screen Time, so it can't simply be deleted. It also blocks deleting apps, in-app purchases, and passcode or account changes.

Does KidWall read my kid's messages?

No. KidWall can't read chats in any app. Its in-app game and chat safety guides show where popular games and apps keep their own parent controls.

Do I need Family Sharing?

Yes. Your child needs a child Apple Account in your Family Sharing group. Apple only allows parental-control apps like KidWall on child accounts in a family.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. KidWall works on iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 18 or later.

Can both parents use it?

Yes. Add a second parent phone with the same family code. You both see the same kids and get the same alerts. One subscription covers you both when you're in the same Family Sharing group.

What happens if I stop paying for Premium?

Kids' devices keep enforcing their current rules. Remote control, alerts, location and multi-parent control need Premium.
